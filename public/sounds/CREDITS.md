# Sound credits

All clips are real recordings from Wikimedia Commons. Each one was trimmed, mixed down to mono, set to 44.1 kHz, filtered (high-pass, plus noise reduction where noted), loudness-matched, faded in and out, and encoded as MP3. Clips cut from CC BY-SA sources are themselves shared under the same CC BY-SA license.
